Hi! Vidhisha here. I've always been the person asking "Oh,how does this work then?"That childhood curiosity never really left—it just got pointed at better things.

These days, I'm fascinated by the intersection of technology, design and human behavior. The tiny details that make a product feel effortless? That's the stuff I notice. One small decision can completely change how something feels to use and I love figuring out why.

I've never been great at fitting into one neat box. I'm interested in a bit of everything and honestly,

AI has only made my curiosity worse (in the best possible way). Questions that would've sent me into a three-hour internet rabbit hole now get answered in seconds which is perfect for my goldfish-level attention span.

Outside of work, I'm usually doodling ideas before they fully make sense, convincing myself I've just thought of the next billion-dollar startup, trying out random hobbies, or getting way too competitive over a badminton game and if I'm not doing any of that, I'm probably reading a book... or watching "just one episode" that somehow turned into four.
